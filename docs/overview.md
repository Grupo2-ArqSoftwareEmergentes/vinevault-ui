# Contextual Overview

- Overview reads `WorkspaceContextService.selectionChanges`, not owner-only organization/space listings. Header, KPI links and quick actions follow the current membership, purpose and selected cellar; no cellar means aggregate only the active space's cellars. Context/authorization changes cancel pending reads and clear old results.
- Four KPIs only: stock quantity sum, registered inventory references (including duplicate names and zero stock), unresolved environmental alerts plus configured Business low-stock warnings (`quantity <= minStockAlert`), and ONLINE sensors with a telemetry reading within 15 minutes. The selected cellar's device binding determines its environmental scope; whole-space views use all device pages. No binding means no linked sensors, not an invented healthy state.
- Failed reads yield unknown (`—`) metrics and a partial-state notice rather than fake zeros. Manual Refresh reloads contextual reads, not quotas or billing. Recent activity includes actual alerts/readings and current low-stock notices; stock links retain the item's own cellar.
- Executive cost is purchase price × current quantity, calculated in exact cents with BigInt for display. Missing prices produce an explicit partial total, or no value at all; no currency or market valuation is inferred. Environmental health is based on actual alerts and reporting status, not hard-coded temperature thresholds.
- `AssistantLauncherService.show()` opens the existing root widget without sending a prompt or creating another conversation. Overview reserves a bottom viewport strip plus content padding so the closed widget trigger cannot cover lower actions while scrolling. The authenticated shell and sidebar retain their shared viewport behavior.
